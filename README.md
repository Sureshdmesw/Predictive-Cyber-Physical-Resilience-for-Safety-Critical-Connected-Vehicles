This research develops and validates a predictive cyber-physical resilience framework for safety-critical connected vehicles that combines temporal AI forecasting, cyber/physical telemetry, uncertainty-aware deterministic decision support, offline Edge-IoT forensic resilience, tamper-evident evidence synchronization, SOC/XDR-compatible integration, and model-grounded explainability—while explicitly preserving the limitations of synthetic cyber telemetry, distribution-shift sensitivity, incomplete OOD detection, and the absence of production vehicle actuation or live Cortex XDR deployment.
